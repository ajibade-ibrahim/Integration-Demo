Three layers, one repository. The framework's loop is graph → agents → actions. This codebase is the data half of the graph layer: it connects to outside systems, reads their records, and keeps one matched copy per customer. Layers 02 and 03 do not exist here at all — the pages below say so in every box where it applies.
For engineers. What a function may write, in what order: src/core/object-schema.ts (FUNCTION_OBJECTS, FUNCTION_WRITE_ORDER). Registration and permissions: src/core/bootstrap.ts plus each provider's scope declaration. Identity resolution: src/core/identity/ and src/core/repositories/identity-repository.ts. Adding a missing slice — a new purpose costs a registry entry, scope declarations, a handler, an object schema and a table — is walked through in docs/architecture.md.
